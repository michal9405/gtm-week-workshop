Why this week

Most companies are bolting AI onto a broken revenue motion.

More tools, more outbound, more activity. Still no system. This week is how you fix that.

The activity

Twelve tools, none connected

Enrichment in one place, sequences in another, the CRM a step behind both. Every team owns a fragment and nobody owns the motion.

The theatre

Volume standing in for strategy

More sends, more meetings booked, more dashboards. The number at the bottom does not move, so the answer is more of the same.

The outcome

Pilots that never became process

The AI experiment worked once, in a demo, on one rep's laptop. It was never wired into how the team actually sells.
